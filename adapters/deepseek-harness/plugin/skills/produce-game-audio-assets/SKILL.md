# Produce Game Audio Assets

Execute one approved audio-asset brief. Produce real deliverables or an honest blocked report; do not substitute a plan for the requested asset work.

## Require inputs

Require the player-facing purpose, asset class, source material or authorized generation method, intended use, target format or delivery contract, acceptance basis, rights authority, localization scope when relevant, and prohibited decisions. If a required source, tool, performer consent, license, or acceptance basis is absent, stop the affected operation and identify the missing authority.

## Perform the work

1. Inspect source identity, recording or generation conditions, edit history, rights evidence, technical condition, and version.
2. Perform only the approved actions: record or synthesize when authorized; select; edit; trim; repair; denoise; de-click; de-breath; layer; design; tune; time; loop; render; normalize against an approved target; split music stems; process dialogue; prepare variations; or create caption and localization metadata.
3. Preserve meaningful variation and gameplay readability. Do not hide clipping, destructive processing, audible seams, phase problems, noise, missing tails, intelligibility loss, or source uncertainty.
4. Export the approved masters and derivatives with deterministic names, versions, channel layout, sample format, loop points, metadata, and checksum or manifest when requested.
5. Audition on the routed listening chain and in the available task context. Label DAW-only or synthetic evidence honestly; do not claim in-engine, device, player, accessibility, localization, or final-mix validation without that evidence.

## Deliver

Return an asset package or bounded synthetic stand-in containing:

- source and rights ledger, including creator or performer, license or consent state, AI assistance and model/tool/version, input ingredients, intended use, and modification chain;
- operation log with tool, settings category, input version, output version, and exceptions;
- masters and derivatives manifest with format, channels, duration, loop or sync metadata, localization/caption identifiers, and integrity evidence;
- listening-QC findings, unresolved defects, integration assumptions, and requested rechecks;
- handoff packet for Audio Director review, interactive-audio integration, Engineering dependencies, and QA validation.

For each delivered output, identify each actual input source and its contribution to any concatenation or layering, with the processing applied. For a trimmed input, record source start and end only when supported; otherwise mark the interval unknown and explain the traceability limitation. For an untrimmed input, say whole source / untrimmed. Output duration is separate and does not establish a source interval. Keep this proportional: a simple untrimmed original needs no inapplicable edit fields. These records do not establish listening quality. Keep traceability handoff recommendations within the approved task and supplied assignments. Mark unspecified preparers or recipients unknown rather than assigning them from general delivery roles; condition any separate listening or integration work on its own task requirement.

## Protect boundaries

- Follow the approved Audio Director brief; do not approve or silently change audio direction, music direction, casting, performance direction, narrative meaning, budget, schedule, platform, quality bar, or release scope.
- Do not replace a Composer, Sound Designer, Dialogue/Voice Director, performer, localization specialist, Legal, Producer, Engineering, QA, or Project Owner when their authority or craft is required.
- Do not invent universal loudness, peak, sample-rate, codec, channel, duration, file-size, or quality thresholds. Use routed targets and report unknowns.
- Do not use, train on, clone, transform, or release a voice or other protected source without routed permission and provenance. Send legal or contractual judgment to the responsible authority.
- Do not implement engine or middleware events, claim runtime mix or performance passes, modify formal Studio baselines, promote the Candidate, deploy, install, stage, commit, or push without separate authorization.
