# Direct Game Art

1. Read the approved Experience Quality Contract or visual objective, target artifact and revision, gameplay/viewing context, platform budget, current visual language, available evidence, and validation authority. If the player promise or visual objective is missing, stop with the missing input instead of inventing a direction.
   - When the user identifies an existing visual result below the requested bar, or an actual check shows a key visual problem remains, and no remedy has been verified for that problem, read and apply [task learning](../studio-orchestrator/references/task-learning.md) before further art-direction work. Reuse applicable verified practice, then inspect relevant experienced practitioners' decisions and first-hand process where needed; translate the comparison into a visual choice and a small check under the approved viewing context. Preserve source/rights and evidence limits, production ownership and existing review gates; familiar small revisions with a verified remedy proceed directly.
2. Translate only the material player promise, observable behaviors, falsifiers, quality floor, and exclusions into visual obligations before selecting an expression. Define purpose, information hierarchy, silhouette, color and non-color cues, material, scale, readability, visual rhythm, expressive tension, accessibility intent, and performance-preservation priorities in gameplay context.
   - For material visual-direction formation or critique, read `references/experience-quality-critique.md`. Select only dimensions relevant to the current promise and stage; do not emit a universal art checklist or cross-project threshold.
3. Produce limited, meaningfully different candidates rather than uncontrolled variations.
4. Track source, license, AI assistance, intended use, and required manual cleanup.
5. Review candidates in gameplay context and against the existing visual language. Separate static, in-engine, runtime, device, performance, accessibility, usability, and player evidence; an earlier layer cannot prove a later one.
   - When batch acceptance depends on actual engine appearance, decide expansion or revision from one representative native sample under the relevant views, distances, motion and material conditions; reuse matching same-version evidence. Offline or front-only evidence cannot establish unseen sides or motion; do not impose motion tests on static 2D work. Without engine authority, hand the check to the existing executor without claiming quality passed; separately authorized source delivery may proceed.
   - Calibrate references and anti-references by the abstract visual relationship being learned or avoided, contextual difference, transfer assumption, provenance/rights state, and protected-expression boundary. Do not copy a recognizable composition, character, symbol, texture, palette arrangement, or style combination.
   - An actionable critique must bind the approved promise, target artifact/version and viewing context, evidence and limits, observed player impact, gap, provisional cause and competing explanation where material, smallest discriminating experiment, falsifier, implementation and validation owners, and recheck trigger. Unbound “more premium,” “cooler,” or “more like the reference” language remains an unverified preference or Owner question.
   - Distinguish local parameter faults with verified remedies from mismatches in the asset, expression method or tool capability. If uncertain, use the smallest discriminating check. Reuse the existing task-learning trigger and controlled-iteration budget; ordinary editing or saving is not a cause-based correction round.
6. Apply [controlled iteration](../studio-orchestrator/references/iteration-and-isolation.md#controlled-iteration) to the same unresolved blocker: after two cause-based failed corrections, or no new useful evidence, pause the failed approach and reassess; new supporting evidence may justify a finite step within existing authority. Ordinary edits are not correction rounds. Preserve any explicitly agreed experiment, cost or permission budget; the general rule does not renew it. Direction, rights and quality-bar decisions remain with their responsible authorities.
7. Do not add a generated asset to the approved asset list without owner approval and technical validation.
8. Art owns the visual contribution and professional critique. Route implementation, numeric budgets, runtime/device/performance measurement, accessibility or usability validation, legal conclusions, and final direction or quality-bar decisions to their responsible authorities.

## Production method ownership and handoff

Art Director owns `direct-game-art`, `produce-game-2d-art`, `produce-game-3d-assets`, `produce-game-realtime-art`, `produce-game-static-character-hair`, and `assemble-game-environments`; `invokable_skills=[]` remains unchanged. Ownership maintains the professional method; it does not grant automatic invocation, production execution, or self-approval. Name the task-scoped executor and downstream receiver before production. Keep AD focused on direction, brief, visual acceptance criteria, critique and handoff.

A production brief binds the selected mode, approved purpose, source revision/hash, exact read/write allowlist, tool route, visual preservation priorities, finite acceptance cases, deliverables, receiver, and evidence invalidation triggers. Route finite native SVG to `produce-game-2d-art`; rigid/static non-character 3D through the mandatory Engineering-owned current preflight consumer to `produce-game-3d-assets`; offline/static VFX or lighting handoff to `produce-game-realtime-art`; editable environment assembly to `assemble-game-environments` through a named scene executor. Environment ownership does not certify a production tool, target platform, or reference match. VFX and lighting require separate receipts and cannot compensate for each other. The `produce-game-static-character-hair` route is limited to an isolated editable static coarse-hair root/crown repair with explicit root/tip identities; the named task-scoped executor may use its manual root-prefix topology recipe when authorized, while its projection helper does not execute that recipe. It does not prove full mesh-topology transfer or broaden to complete character, material/lookdev, rig, animation, engine/runtime, device/player, or performance work.

Engineering receives technical interfaces and owns implementation, numeric/runtime/device/performance validation; QA verifies; Reviewer independently reviews; Project Owner adopts. No permanent art-production or Technical Artist Agent is introduced. Preserve each method\'s literal evidence ceiling and raw failures; method registration is not proof of production quality or runtime readiness.
