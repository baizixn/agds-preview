# Competitor Research — Conditional Reference

Use only after the core route records `eligible_for_research=true`. This reference contains the low-frequency research procedure only. After eligibility, apply the source-judgment and comparison checks in [domain learning](domain-learning.md), reusing checks already performed; the explicit competitor gate and packet remain authoritative.

1. **Freeze the research brief before collection.** Record one decision question; target player and use context; approved stage and constraints; hypothesis being discriminated; downstream decision and recipient; in-scope and excluded comparison dimensions; evidence cutoff; and validation authority. A material change to question, target/use context, or downstream decision returns to the core route for a new bounded task/packet—never silent scope expansion.
2. **Bound the comparison question and initial source coverage.** Select items able to expose relevant similarity, materially different methods, failures or competing explanations, with each inclusion reason tied to the question. Within the same approved question and cutoff, extend or replace this initial set when a material counterexample, shared source origin or missing process could change the decision; preserve the reason and contrary evidence. Finalize the inspected set and actual counts in the returned packet, rather than claiming the initial list was exhaustive. Do not select by popularity or keep adding examples until a preferred answer appears. For each item capture title, exact version/build when available, observation/publication date, platform, material region/service context, observed mode and progression/account state, session/sample limits, relevant settings, and source locator. Mark unavailable identity fields; do not guess.
3. **Keep claim-level evidence classes separate.** Label every material claim `direct_observation`, `official_statement`, `independent_third_party`, or `model_inference`, with source, date, applicable context, claim scope, and limits. For competitor claims that may have changed, verify against and cite current first-party or otherwise authoritative primary sources. Marketing language, review sentiment, and secondary summaries alone do not satisfy this freshness check. Claim scope must separate artifact/presentation existence from player impact. Direct observation states only what the bounded artifact/session showed; official statements establish publisher claims, not player evidence; third-party evidence retains method, sample, recency, and access limits; model inference remains hypothesis. Conflicting, stale, incomparable, or insufficient evidence is `Inconclusive`, with the exact missing evidence.
4. **Build causal teardown, not a feature list.** Trace `rule -> player-available information -> player choice -> cost/risk -> strategy or resulting dynamic -> feedback -> hypothesized experience`. At every link distinguish observation from inference, cite evidence, state its limit, name the nearest plausible competing explanation, and expose broken or unobserved links. Resemblance, shared labels, correlation, sentiment, or popularity is not a causal chain.
5. **Map only relevant time scales.** Describe applicable moment-to-moment, encounter/session, and longer progression/return loops, including the state, reward, risk, or learning passed across scales. Justify `not_applicable`. Never infer long-term outcomes from one short session or local interaction without evidence for the cross-scale link.
6. **Test load-bearing mechanism against alternatives.** For each mechanism claimed to carry a strategy or experience, state the counterfactual change expected if it is removed, weakened, delayed, or isolated. Compare it with at least one material alternative cause allowed by routed context. If evidence cannot discriminate them, preserve competing hypotheses rather than selecting a winner.
7. **Bound transfer and protected expression.** A transferable principle must state donor context, target-fit conditions, dependencies, failure conditions, contextual differences, and evidence status. Do not transfer distinctive wording, audiovisual assets, characters, narrative expression, layouts, level composition, content, or tuning. Transform abstract learning rather than copy expression. Similarity is inspiration, not authorization.
8. **Specify the minimum discriminating experiment.** Use the smallest meaningful controlled comparison that distinguishes the transfer hypothesis from its nearest competing explanation, normally changing one factor. If separating coupled mechanisms destroys the behavior under study, explain why the bundle is indivisible for this question and preserve the inability to attribute its effect to any single factor. Predeclare expected player behavior, observation method, sample/setup limits, measure, falsifier, decision rule, and the core-defined explicit `Adjust` and `Stop` conditions. Bind the comparison to the brief's decision and competing explanations: identify both conditions and the meaningful invariants, separating routed constraints from design choices. When the brief leaves alternatives open, explain what each would discriminate; do not treat different baselines or causal questions as interchangeable. A changed comparison needs a stated evidence or assumption reason, not merely another presentation order. Preserve legitimate alternative designs and do not force a single-factor test of an indivisible bundle. If no bounded experiment discriminates the claim, return the evidence gap rather than expanding content or recommending a transfer.
9. **Apply evidence-insufficiency and stopping rules.** Do not claim fun, player success, retention, commercial causality, target-context fit, or likely transfer from popularity, unattributed public metrics, marketing language, review sentiment, surface similarity, a single bounded session, or model synthesis. Stop when relevant alternatives and credible challenges have been considered, critical claims have inspectable support and transfer limits, and further collection is unlikely to change the bounded decision; or when the remaining discriminator needs player, telemetry, legal, market, platform, or specialist evidence. A time, cutoff or access limit does not establish sufficient learning: preserve the unresolved question and narrow or defer its recommendation. Also stop for scope drift, project-specific contamination, protected-expression risk, or a core authority gate. Return `Not Run` or `Inconclusive`, the exact gap, and smallest evidence request; uncertainty is not a recommendation.
10. **Return one compact research packet.** Include frozen brief; comparison/version matrix; claim-level evidence ledger; causal chain; multi-time-scale loop map; load-bearing mechanism and competing hypotheses; counterfactual; transfer fit/failure conditions and protected-expression boundary; minimum experiment; evidence cutoff and current evidence states; unresolved questions; specialist-owned dependencies; and recheck triggers.
