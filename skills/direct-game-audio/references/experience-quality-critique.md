# Audio experience-quality critique

Use this reference only for a material audio-direction or in-context critique task. Start from the routed Experience Quality Contract; the dimensions below are a router, not a scorecard.

## Map the promise to an audio obligation

For each selected obligation, record the target player and use context, gameplay state, bound core feeling or observable behavior, falsifier and quality floor, explicit exclusion, target event/asset/system and revision, listening chain, evidence class, and decision authority. Describe what audio must help the player know, feel, or do before choosing an instrument, timbre, melody, rhythm, sample, or processing technique.

Classify the semantic role as primary information, redundant support, interaction feedback, emotional support, environmental continuity, or intentional silence. Define fallback when the cue is missing, masked, non-spatial, unavailable in a language, or unsupported by a device.

## Select material dimensions

- **Information clarity:** meaning, source, direction, urgency, and actionable response under real concurrency; exclude cases where vision alone creates a false positive.
- **Interaction feedback:** trigger, response, outcome, failure, interruption, and distinction among similar actions or states.
- **Emotion:** contribution to the approved feeling and its time course; polish or “coolness” is not player-emotion evidence.
- **Dynamics:** intensity, density, hierarchy, and protection of critical speech, warnings, and action feedback as gameplay state changes.
- **Variation:** reduced perceptible repetition without losing event identity, timing learning, or predictability over the approved session shape.
- **Spatial duty:** source binding, direction, distance, occlusion, environment, and non-spatial fallback; authoring preview is not target-device evidence.
- **Mix interaction:** masking, inverted priorities, excessive ducking, category loss, control separation, and failure behavior under system concurrency.
- **Music transition:** entry, exit, escalation, de-escalation, interruption, recovery, and failure timing relative to gameplay meaning.
- **Fatigue risk:** sustained stimulation, abruptness, warning frequency, lack of silence, and inadequate variation across expected duration. Route actual-player and affected-player evidence when required.

## Calibrate without copying

For every reference or anti-reference, retain creator/performer, source, rights or consent state, AI/provenance fields, the abstract functional relationship, expected player consequence, contextual difference, transfer assumption, transformation rule, and recognizable expression that must not be copied. Unknown-rights material supports limited exploration only. Reference quality is not asset rights, runtime, listening, or acceptance evidence.

## Write one actionable critique

Record a stable critique ID; event/asset/system and revision; gameplay state, build, language and listening chain; Experience Contract and promise binding; semantic role and priority; evidence identity/class/limits; observed player impact and gap; provisional root cause plus credible alternative; smallest discriminating experiment and decision rule; falsifier; unaffected behavior and fallback to protect; execution owner; independent validation owner; final decision owner; and recheck trigger.

Use `Not Run`, `Inconclusive`, `Supports`, or `Falsifies` only for the routed audio contribution assessment. These states are not QA Pass/Fail, listener generalization, rights approval, or Owner acceptance. When the artifact, build, gameplay state, listening chain, contract, or evidence authority changes, narrow or invalidate the prior assessment.
