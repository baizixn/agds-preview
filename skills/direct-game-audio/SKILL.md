# Direct Game Audio

1. Start from the approved Experience Quality Contract or audio objective, intended use, target event/asset/system and revision, gameplay/listening context, player information, emotion, interaction feedback, spatial role, platform budget, available evidence, and validation authority. If the player promise, audio objective, or intended use is missing, stop instead of inventing a direction.
2. Separate required gameplay cues from atmosphere and music. Select workflow depth from the approved goal: bounded audition selection, retention, or targeted cue/transition judgment; or material direction formation/system critique. Apply the following obligations only as needed for that goal; professional coverage is not authorization for additional work.
3. Define the player-side audio obligation and semantic role before selecting expression.
   - For bounded audition judgment, reuse adequate supplied listening evidence to decide suitability, retention, or the smallest targeted alternative. Identify only missing evidence that could change that decision. Measurements and metadata do not substitute for listening; do not require remakes to meet a quota or merely because production is available.
   - When designing or critiquing system behavior, define the relevant trigger, priority, variation, spatialization, mixing, transition, fatigue risk, and failure or fallback behavior.
   - For material audio-direction formation or system critique, read `references/experience-quality-critique.md`. Select only dimensions relevant to the current promise, gameplay state, and stage; do not emit a universal audio checklist or numeric threshold.
4. Track source, license, AI assistance, edits, and intended use for external assets, retaining supplied records and their limits. Audition suitability is not rights approval and does not itself require new confirmation. Authorized sourcing, modification, integration, or distribution requires the applicable provenance and rights/consent basis; unresolved rights block that use. Do not invent legal conclusions.
5. Review in context at the depth required for the current decision; avoid judging isolated audio as final game quality. Separate brief, isolated audition, authoring tool, editor, runtime, target device, performance, accessibility, localization, usability, and player/listener evidence.
   - A bounded audition recommendation binds purpose, target/revision, listening evidence, and limits; it does not require the full critique below.
   - When using references or anti-references, calibrate functional relationships such as information hierarchy, event timing, contrast, masking control, density, transition grammar, repetition, spatial duty, or emotional trajectory. Do not copy recognizable melody, signature rhythm, distinctive timbral combination, performance identity, sample, dialogue expression, mix fingerprint, or branded structure.
   - For material direction/system critique, bind the approved promise, target event/asset/system and gameplay/listening context, evidence and limits, observed player impact, gap, provisional cause and competing explanation where material, smallest discriminating experiment, falsifier, execution and validation owners, relevant fallback requirement, and recheck trigger. “Sounds better,” “more epic,” or “like the reference” is not actionable without those bindings.
6. Submit a scoped recommendation, Candidate cue plan, or asset set and wait for owner approval before promotion.
7. Audio Direction owns audio obligations and professional critique. Route asset production and interactive integration to Audio Production, code and budgets to Engineering, evidence validity to QA, scope/resources to Producer, player-promise changes to Game Design/Owner, and final audio direction, quality bar, rights acceptance, or release decisions to their authorities.
