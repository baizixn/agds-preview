# Visual experience-quality critique

Use this reference only for a material visual-direction or in-context critique task. Start from the routed Experience Quality Contract; the dimensions below are a router, not a scorecard.

## Map the promise to a visual obligation

For each selected visual obligation, record the target player and use context, gameplay moment and player task, bound core feeling or observable behavior, applicable falsifier and quality floor, explicit exclusion, target artifact/revision, viewing conditions, evidence class, and decision authority. Describe what the player must perceive or do before choosing a visual technique.

## Select material dimensions

- **Information hierarchy:** first read, action information, decoration, and competition from UI, VFX, characters, and background under motion and occlusion.
- **Distinctiveness:** silhouette, shape, scale, value, material, motion, and non-color cues at the approved distance and context.
- **Visual rhythm:** density, rest, anticipation, impact, recovery, and focal movement across the gameplay beat.
- **Style coherence:** shape language, proportion, edge treatment, value structure, material response, and motion grammar; a deviation needs gameplay meaning.
- **Expressive tension:** composition, pose, scale, light, material, and timing in service of the approved feeling without trading away clarity or comfort.
- **Accessibility intent:** non-color cues, text and object clarity, motion/flash risk, safe-area and localization pressure. Route formal criteria and affected-player evidence to their authorities.
- **Performance preservation:** name the visual information that must survive LOD, mip, transparency, lighting, VFX, UI, or other degradation; Engineering and QA own budgets and measurements.

## Calibrate without copying

For every reference or anti-reference, retain source/provenance and rights status, the abstract relationship being learned or avoided, the expected player consequence, contextual difference, transfer assumption, project transformation rule, and the recognizable expression that must not be copied. Popularity and resemblance do not prove fit. Unknown-rights material is exploration input, not approval-ready production input.

## Write one actionable critique

Record a stable critique ID; artifact/version/stage; gameplay and viewing context; Experience Contract and promise binding; evidence identity, class, date, limits and invalidation; observed player impact and gap; provisional root cause plus credible alternative; smallest discriminating experiment and controlled variables; falsifier; unchanged behavior to protect; Art priority; implementation owner; validation owner; final decision owner; and recheck trigger.

Use `Not Run`, `Inconclusive`, `Supports`, or `Falsifies` only for the routed visual contribution assessment. These states are not QA Pass/Fail or Owner acceptance. When context, artifact, contract, or evidence authority changes, narrow or invalidate the prior assessment rather than refreshing its date.
