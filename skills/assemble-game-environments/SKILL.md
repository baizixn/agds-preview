# Assemble Game Environments

Make a coherent, editable space that can be received in the requested engine scene. Art Director owns this method and judges visual intent; a named scene executor assembles it, Design owns player-space requirements, specialist executors produce assets/effects, Engineering integrates and measures, QA verifies, and Owner adopts. The method does not certify every production tool, target platform or reference match.

## Choose the depth and establish the route

Identify reconstruction, original assembly or local revision. Bind the approved quality target, reference scope, writable root, protected content, source revisions, dependencies/rights and required evidence. Preserve current authorization. For a small placement correction with settled style/camera, reuse those decisions and check only affected views, bounds/contact and behavior. Do not require a new world bible or full-scene review for it.

Before any scene write, verify the actual tool can read/write the named target and preserve its native dependencies. Record the observed version/connection and one relevant read or existing receipt. A tool listed in a catalog is not an execution receipt. If no engine route works, do analysis/tool discovery; use offline assembly only if that separate route is verified and authorized, and label it offline-only. Do not invent execution or expand protected settings to remove a blocker.

For substantial reconstruction read [spatial reconstruction](references/reference-reconstruction.md). For multi-region style or specialist water/VFX read [world style and specialist handoffs](references/world-and-specialists.md). Read [production assembly](references/production-assembly.md) when decomposing assets for production, consolidation, collision or effects. Use [scene craft](references/scene-craft.md) for terrain, kits and distribution; [Unity delivery](references/unity-delivery.md) only for Unity. Research provenance is in [research basis](references/research-basis.md).

## Work through evidence-bearing decisions

**D1 — Bound the visual language.** Read the approved whole-world direction before interpreting a local region. Separate cross-region hypotheses, observed local variation and unknown coverage. Shape language, proportion, palette/value hierarchy, material detail density, light and water/sky/effect behavior all matter; do not equate “stylized” with one polygon count. With only one region, produce a provisional local reading and identify the missing contrast reference; continue bounded local work without claiming the whole world is established. Use the compact style contract in the specialist reference.

**D2 — Resolve space cheaply.** Make one shared anchor/relationship map, relevant terrain sections and matched coarse views, rather than a list of props. Record observations, hypotheses and unknowns separately. Solve camera/projection/scale before manufacturing fine geometry. Different views must use the same scene geometry; visible contradictions cannot be averaged away. An image-space ratio is not a true model dimension. Use a silhouette proxy, not a detailed mesh, to test competing pose/depth hypotheses.

**D3 — Decide whether refinement may proceed.** Before detailing the affected mass or asset, issue a short spatial receipt: reference IDs and camera revision; shared anchor relationships; all essential comparison views; accepted tolerances and their source; residual contradictions/unknowns; visual judge and permitted next work. Inspect the actual coarse captures, not just the receipt fields. All essential observed relationships must hold within the agreed evidence precision. Missing depth may permit explicitly provisional hidden geometry; unresolved essential scale, shoreline or pose blocks dependent detail. Freeze cameras after calibration; retain old and new receipts if recalibration becomes necessary. Broad color and provisional light may be tested in the coarse scene to expose shape/light coupling.

**D4 — Give specialist work a usable interface.** Decide reuse, proxy or production per required feature. Bind editable source, units/pivot/contact surfaces, material/pipeline assumptions, event/time behavior when relevant, acceptance views and receiver. Keep three receiving responsibilities explicit: specialist source production; assembly of spatial/contact relationships; Engineering import/integration and actual cost measurement when required. Record a missing person or tool as unassigned/unverified under that responsibility, rather than omitting the receiver or inventing one. No available executor/tool means a named gap and a blocked dependent feature, not silent replacement by primitives. Assembly does not expand a rigid-asset Skill into character production, or an offline VFX pack into runtime proof. Named specialists may be the same person/agent only with a verified scoped capability and distinct evidence.

**D5 — Reassess a failed approach and respect explicit budgets.** Apply [controlled iteration](../studio-orchestrator/references/iteration-and-isolation.md#controlled-iteration) to the same unresolved visible relationship. After two cause-driven failed corrections, or no new useful evidence, pause that approach; preserve the results, recheck assumptions and actual views, consult necessary source or reliable references, and adjust the method. New supporting evidence may justify a finite step within existing authority. Files, commits and ordinary edits are not rounds; changing the hypothesis, tool, executor, failure category or name does not erase the failed attempts or supply new evidence. Independent advice is optional, not a required review chain. Independent unaffected work may continue. Ask for a specific Owner decision only if no viable method remains or scope, architecture tradeoffs, cost, authority or quality must change. An explicitly agreed experiment/correction count, cost or permission budget still binds: when exhausted, stop its covered trials and hand off the evidence; further trials require authority covering the renewed budget, reusing such authority if already given. General permission to build a slice does not renew an exhausted explicit budget.

When D5 permits a next step, trial a representative join/contact/material before replication. Choose the largest mismatch and record hypothesis, variables, predicted change, disconfirming view/time and result. If contradicted, reclassify the cause without erasing the failure history. An explained coupled change may form one cause-driven correction when the relationship is indivisible; it remains subject to any explicit budget. Do not repeat global noise/scatter/angle changes without causal discrimination or lower the agreed quality target.

**D6 — Report only the evidence earned.** Keep reference coverage, spatial/visual fidelity, artistic coherence, editable-file/reopen integrity, engine runtime/input route, actual target performance and cross-region transfer separate. Preserve supplied Fail, Inconclusive and Not Run distinctly, including in a short answer: an observed mismatch is not missing coverage. Append a supported correction to a prior diagnosis without rewriting its historical evidence. Motion samples establish sampled change only; hashes/counts establish integrity/inventory only. An essential visual failure blocks a fidelity claim even when runtime and files pass. Use fixed-view comparisons plus the requested player route; profile real frame time on the named build/hardware only when in scope. A new region must test the invariant style against different geometry/composition; recoloring the same layout is not transfer.

Deliver native sources and dependencies, the decision receipts already used (not a second report), captures and raw run evidence at the requested tier, remaining blockers with the smallest resolving action and named receiver. Review recommendation is not Owner approval; no registration or release follows automatically.

In that handoff, distinguish a completed production operation from a verified artifact property. “Merged”, “exported” or “saved” alone does not prove editability, usability, intact materials or successful reopening. State those checks as unverified until an actual receipt supports them; do not fill them from the intended delivery format. For each proposed next action, name the responsible function (an assignee may remain pending) so a short answer still has an executable receiver.

Before a substantial scene write, use the short [execution record](references/execution-record.md): who decides the spatial specification, who writes the scene, and which specification is being applied. Missing specifications stay pending with no scene write. The designated writer checks the record before using the engine, applies the referenced specification and returns actual transform/save/reopen evidence. One verified executor may own both decisions and writing. For an already approved small move, one sentence naming the executor, approved change and result handoff is sufficient; do not add people, records or approval steps unnecessarily.

For a substantial reconstruction, even a short next-step answer or handoff excerpt must retain this decision core (reuse the working record; no duplicate report):

- **Spatial release:** reference/view IDs, camera revision, inspected coarse evidence, tolerance source and visual judge; current gate and permitted next work. If these do not yet exist, mark each missing item pending and the dependent detail unreleased. Do not fabricate a passed receipt just to fill the fields.
- **Production receivers:** specialist source, scene/contact assembly and Engineering import/integration/cost responsibilities, with assignment/tool gaps. Missing production tools do not erase downstream responsibilities; precise asset formats and measured costs can remain pending.
- **Correction disposition:** the unresolved relationship, failed approaches and any explicit used/allowed budget; state whether the approach is paused for reassessment, a finite next step is supported by new evidence and current authority, or a specific decision/budget renewal is needed. Retain the actual before/after views, camera revisions, anchors, variables and results, not only a failure summary. Route a transform/contact correction to scene assembly; route an evidenced source-mesh change to asset production. If the cause is unresolved, keep those conditional receivers explicit without assigning a source defect. A hypothetical future experiment must not read as permission to run now.

This core applies to substantial reconstruction with those dependencies, not an already-settled single-object placement. Scale optional detail to the next decision; write planned evidence as planned, never as an actual execution receipt.
